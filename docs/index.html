    How this works
    • Every few hours a script sets each store as the default store on walmart.ca and iga.net, exactly as the "make this my store" button does, then reads carrot juice availability for that store.
    • Walmart reports a quantity: the units it will sell online from that store. A restock is when a product goes from out of stock to in stock; a top-up is when the quantity jumps by 3 or more without selling out first.
    • IGA only says whether a product is available at the selected store, with no quantity. IGA Saint-Charles does not take online orders, so IGA publishes no stock for it.
    • Times are when the change was detected; the change happened some time since the previous check.
    • Online stock is the stores' own figure, but it can lag the shelf by a few hours, and Walmart holds back a small buffer.
